Create a piggy bank for each of your savings goals, whether it's a vacation, a new gadget, or an emergency fund. Each piggy bank can have its own currency, savings target, deadline, and notes.

Add or withdraw money as needed and record every transaction. You can also include notes with individual transactions, which makes it easier to remember what a particular deposit or withdrawal was for.

Piggsy isn't tied to your actual bank balance. Think of it as a tool for visualizing your savings goals and tracking how much you've set aside for each. You don't need to move money between accounts or change your banking habits to use it.

Set deadlines to help you keep an eye on your goals, and receive notifications when they're reached. You will keep your data safe with automatic encrypted backups.

Piggsy works entirely offline. Your savings data stays on your device.
