Piggsy is a simple way to keep track of your savings, all in one place.

Whether you're saving for a trip, a new gadget, or building an emergency fund, this app offers you a space for multiple piggy banks in your preferred currency. Flexible deadlines and notes will help you stay organized. You're in control of everything.

## Features

- **Multiple piggy banks**
  Create as many goals as you need. Each has its own optional target, currency, optional deadline, and notes.

- **Track deposits and withdrawals**
  Add or withdraw money whenever you need to keep your balance up to date. Each piggy bank is your premise and keeps a full transaction history with notes for individual transactions.

- **Deadlines**
  Pick a specific day and time in the calendar and get notified when the deadline is reached.

- **Automatic encrypted backups**
  Pick a folder and let Piggsy export data in the background, so your savings goals and transaction history don't get lost.

- **Private by design**
  Biometric lock, no analytics and no INTERNET permission. Your data stays on your device.
